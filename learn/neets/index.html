Navy Electricity and Electronics Training Series

NEETS

The whole series the Navy taught its electronics technicians from, all twenty-four modules, from atoms and Ohm's law in Module 1 through fiber optics in Module 24. It is written to teach a beginner and it is public domain. Read it in the browser or pull the PDFs down and keep them.

Where it comes from

About these files

NEETS was produced by the U.S. Navy to train its electronics technicians. As a work of the United States Government it carries no copyright and is free to copy and share. These are the standard PDF modules, mirrored here so the whole set stays in one place and stays up. Nothing here has been altered.

Some passages reflect the equipment and practice of the era they were written in. Treated as a grounding in the physics and the fundamentals, which do not change, the series still teaches the subject as clearly as anything since.
