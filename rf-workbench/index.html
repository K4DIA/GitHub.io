RFWorkbench

The radio engineer's bench, all on one page.

Link budgets, Fresnel clearance, path-loss models, matching networks, transmission lines and circuit calculators. Every result recalculates as you type. Units can be switched per field, values accept SI suffixes like 4k7, 2.2n or 150M, and the ☆ on any card pins it to your bench.
