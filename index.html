Field Record

A printable logbook built around the outing rather than the contact. Put in your callsign, your license class and your state, choose what goes in it, and take away a PDF built for your station. It runs in your browser, so nothing you type is sent anywhere.

  • Band plan drawn for your license class
  • Quick reference cards for fifteen radios
  • Your repeaters, or ruled grids to fill in
  • 1 to 60 deployments, four pages each
