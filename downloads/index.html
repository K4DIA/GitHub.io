Before you print

How to print it

All of it is 8.5 by 11 portrait with a wide inner margin, sized for a three ring binder and a desktop printer. No trimming, no bleed, no print shop.

  1. 01
    Duplex on the long edge Or print single sided if your printer will not duplex. The margin is wide enough either way.
  2. 02
    Actual size, not fit to page Scaling shrinks the punch margin and the holes will eat the log columns.
  3. 03
    Punch the left edge There is 0.90 inch of clear margin on the binding side of every page for exactly this.
  4. 04
    Color if you have it The band chart uses color to show what your license class may transmit on. It still reads in black and white, but color is faster with cold hands.
